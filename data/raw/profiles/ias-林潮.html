Welcome

UC Santa Cruz is a world-renowned leader in astronomy and astrophysics. We are innovators of telescope technology, creators of new research methods, and pioneers in using the world’s fastest supercomputers.  From planets to stars to galaxies to cosmology, we have made some of the biggest discoveries in the field and have received some of the highest honors.

Undergraduate students have the opportunity to be involved in this cutting-edge research—a defining feature of our programs. The Astronomy & Astrophysics Department offers dynamic degree programs tailored to your goals. Explore our graduate and undergraduate programs to find which path is right for you.  We are committed to support a diverse community and an inclusive environment that promotes innovation and scientific excellence.
